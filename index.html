Privacy Policy

Omega Habits Β· by Belurien Forge Β· Effective date: 21 October 2026

Omega Habits ("the app") is a habit and streak tracker made by Belurien Forge. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: we don't collect any.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your habits, check-ins, streaks, badges and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to any server we run.

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your Google account backup. That backup is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy, not by us.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the daily reminder you choose.
  • Alarms (optional): only if you tap "Add daily alarm to my Clock app". The app then creates an alarm in your phone's Clock app, which you can edit or delete there at any time.

Links

You can attach a link to a habit. When you tap it, it opens in your browser or another app. Those websites and apps have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

Deleting your data

Because your data exists only on your device, you control it completely. Delete any habit inside the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example a future optional sync or purchase feature, we will update this page and change the effective date above before the change takes effect.
